WIREFORM JIG procedural forms · motion · sound

Sound is generated live from the form — its shape, spin and breathing drive pitch, filter and rhythm.

How to use: press Shuffle ~20 times, ★ Keep the ones you like, click a keep to restore it, then lock groups you want to protect and tweak by hand. Space shuffle · F keep · P play/stop sound. Drag on the canvas to orbit manually.

What this is: a parametric wireframe system — the same family as your references: lat/long grids, hourglasses (hyperboloids), tori, Möbius loops, spiky stars and wave sheets, drawn as pure lines on black.

System: a surface f(u,v) → deformed (twist / pinch / bumps / wave) → morphed toward a second form → rotated in 3D → perspective-projected. Lines are sampled from that surface, so every slider is geometry.

Sound: the surface is also the score. Each sequencer step samples a vertex of the current form — its X becomes a scale degree, its depth becomes octave, twist becomes filter, spin becomes swing/LFO. Change the shape and the music changes with it.

Analogue chain: saw/square oscillators → resonant low-pass → tape saturation (waveshaper) → feedback delay → generated-impulse reverb → compressor, plus tape hiss. No samples, no files — everything is synthesised from the drawing.
